# GRIP-MPC

<p class="publication-meta"><span class="publication-meta__year">2026 – </span><span>Tongji University</span><span class="publication-meta__status">· Ongoing</span></p>

<div class="archive__item-excerpt" markdown="1">

**Overview.** A novel End-to-End RL-based vehicle motion control framework, motivated by [AC-MPC](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11301631), featuring a self-evolving, differentiable, and interpretable end-to-end control architecture. The word "GRIP-MPC" is an abbreviation of *Guided Recurrent Inference Policy for Model Predictive Control*.

**Results & Demonstrations.**

This project is sponsored mainly by Dongfeng Automobile Company.

Results will be published after the paper is accepted and the R&D project is no longer subject to the NDA.

# Learning-based Control

<p class="publication-meta"><span class="publication-meta__year">2025 – </span><span>Tongji University</span><span class="publication-meta__status">· Ongoing</span></p>

<div class="archive__item-excerpt" markdown="1">

**Overview.** A survey of learning-based motion control for intelligent vehicles from a closed-loop control perspective. The review organizes the field into Model Learning, Policy Learning, and Safety Constraint Learning, examining their roles in control design and the evidence needed to assess performance and safety.

**Results & Demonstrations.**

A survey paper [*Learning-Based Motion Control for Intelligent Vehicles: A Taxonomy from a Closed-Loop Control Perspective*]({{ base_path }}/publication/survey-lbc), has been submitted to *IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems*.

</div>

</section>

# Zonotopic State Estimation & Fault Diagnosis

<p class="publication-meta"><span class="publication-meta__year">2022 - 2025</span><span>Jiangnan University</span></p>

<div class="archive__item-excerpt" markdown="1">

**Overview.** Undergraduate research on zonotopic state estimation and fault diagnosis under bounded uncertainty. The work combined filter design for time-delay systems with a lithium-ion battery test platform, covering algorithm development, hardware implementation, and experimental validation.

**Results & Demonstrations.**

- Multiple journal papers have been published in *IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics* and *IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement*.
- Undergraduate thesis on zonotopic state estimation for time-delay systems was recognized as an Outstanding Undergraduate Thesis.
